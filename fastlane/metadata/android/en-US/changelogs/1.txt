First public release (beta).

Offline, encrypted storage for bank card PINs, login PINs, phone PINs, passwords
and bank account details. Master PIN with password fallback and optional
biometric unlock, timed reveal, blocked screenshots, auto-wiping clipboard,
auto-lock on backgrounding, dark mode, and password-protected encrypted backups.

Automatic backups: an encrypted copy of the previous state is kept on the phone
before every change, so a deleted entry can be restored from Settings.

Wrong guesses are rate-limited, and the wait can't be cut short by force-quitting,
rebooting or changing the phone's clock.

Marked beta because the app is newly released and iOS is written for but
untested. Keep an exported backup.
