Pin and sort.

Pin the entries you use most: the pin button on an entry's screen moves it to a
Pinned section at the top of My PINs. Tap it again to put it back in its bank.

A new sort button orders the list A–Z, by recently used or by recently added,
and remembers your choice. The list is now A–Z by default.

Pins and recently-used times don't use up your automatic backups, which are
kept for real edits.
